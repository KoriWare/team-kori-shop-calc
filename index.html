Quick start

  1. Pick Radial or Axial mode with the top toggle.
  2. Set tool Ø, engagement (ae/% or ap + κ), desired chipload, flutes, and RPM.
  3. Read heff, the feed multiplier, and adjusted feed in the big numbers.
  4. Flip in | mm in the header anytime — it rewrites the whole board (angles, Z, RPM stay put).
  5. Open Convert under Results for SFM↔rpm / ipt↔ipm, then tap Use this… to push into the calc.

Radial mode

Side-milling / circular engagement. Factor = √((ae/D)·(2−ae/D)) when ae/D < 1, else 1. Light ae thins the chip — raise table feed (Compensate ON) so effective chip thickness matches your desired ipt. Diagram: tool circle with shaded ae wedge.

Axial mode

Lead / entering angle thinning. Factor = sin(κ) where κ is the lead angle in degrees (90° ≈ 1, 45° ≈ 0.707, 15° high-feed ≈ 0.259). Inputs: Ø (for surface speed), ap (axial DOC), κ (presets or free), ipt, Z, RPM. Diagram: side-view depth + lead-angle geometry.

Convert + units

in | mm lives in the header and flips every length field and label on the board (×25.4). Convert is a violet accordion under Results: SFM↔RPM needs Ø; ipt↔IPM needs Z + RPM. In mm mode it shows m/min ↔ rpm and mm/tooth ↔ mm/min. Gold stays on DEMO $ only — never next to Convert.

DEMO costs only

DEMO costs only. Blank $, burden rate, and scrap totals are sample numbers so you can see how scrap burns cash. They are not anyone’s real shop rates. Toggle the panel off if you only want the thinning math.

What’s not in this build

What’s not here yet. Setup / PM due board and arrive-by timers stay parked for a later tab — not missing, just not shipped.
