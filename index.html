Quick start

  1. Set tool Ø, ae (or % of D), desired ipt, flutes, and RPM.
  2. Read heff, the feed multiplier, and adjusted IPM in the big numbers.
  3. Turn Compensate on for light radial cuts (ae well under half the tool diameter).
  4. Tap Copy adjusted feed when you like the numbers.

How to read the diagram

Engagement diagram. The circle is your tool. The shaded wedge is radial engagement (ae) — how much of the diameter is in the cut. At light ae the chip gets thinner than the ipt you typed, so table feed has to go up to hold chip load. Thick→thin arrow = that effect. Flip Compensate when ae is well under half the tool diameter; the violet badge is your feed multiplier. Big numbers = rpm / ipm / adjusted feed — designed to read on a shop phone.

DEMO costs only

DEMO costs only. Blank $, burden rate, and scrap totals are sample numbers so you can see how scrap burns cash. They are not anyone’s real shop rates. Toggle the panel off if you only want the thinning math.
